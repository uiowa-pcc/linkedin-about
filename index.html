Build Your LinkedIn About Section

Write a LinkedIn About section that sounds like you. You might focus on what motivates you, your background, your skills, or a mix of these. A few meaningful sentences can be enough.

No login required. This tool works in your browser and does not send your writing anywhere. Copying your draft into an AI service is your choice.

Step 1 of 3

Choose where to start

Your LinkedIn About section can focus on one aspect of who you are. Choose a starting point, then decide whether adding anything else would strengthen your introduction. Adding more is always optional.

What would you like people to know about you?

What Interests Me

I'm curious about how schools and community programs help young people feel connected and supported. I enjoy learning what creates a sense of belonging and why it matters.

What I've Done & Learned

I'm a psychology student at Iowa. Volunteering at an after-school program introduced me to youth development and helped me discover how much I enjoy working directly with students.

What I Bring

I enjoy making complex information easier to understand. Through class projects, I've practiced researching questions, organizing details, and sharing ideas in ways other people can use.

Fictional examples. Each takes a different approach; none needs to cover every part of someone's story.

Would another detail make your introduction stronger?

You can finish with just your starting focus. Select any additions that help, and leave the rest out.

Keep it focused. One topic can make a strong About section. Add something else only if it helps someone better understand who you are or what you bring.

Make the draft yours

Your draft keeps your main focus central and places related details together. A focused paragraph about one topic is completely fine. If you included several ideas, read through the transitions and adjust anything that doesn't sound natural.

Want to add more detail? (Optional ideas)
  • Could you be a little more specific about what interests you or why?
  • Is there something about your background or an experience you'd like people to know?
  • Would mentioning a skill, perspective, or future curiosity make your introduction stronger?

You do not need to answer all—or any—of these. A concise, focused About section is a valid finished result.
