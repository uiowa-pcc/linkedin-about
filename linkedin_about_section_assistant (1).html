Build My LinkedIn About Section

Use the prompts that help you tell your story. Your About section can focus on what has shaped you professionally, where you want to go, or both.

You do not need to complete every prompt. Choose the pieces that help someone understand something meaningful about you professionally. One strong idea developed with detail is better than trying to include everything.

Step 1 · Choose your story

What story do you want your About section to tell?

Your About section does not need to cover your whole background. Start with the direction that feels most useful right now.

Choose one starting point
No perfect answer: this choice only helps organize your thinking. You can change direction while you draft.

Step 2 · Build the story

Use only the prompts that help

You can answer several briefly or choose one or two and go deeper. The goal is enough detail to sound specific and genuine.

Try not to fill space just because a box is here. If one experience or direction tells your story well, give that more detail and leave the rest blank.

Step 3 · Make it yours

Shape your starting draft

Use this as a starting point. Keep the parts that tell your story well, expand what matters, and remove anything that feels unnecessary or unlike you.

My About draft

Before you use it, check:

  • Does this sound like me?
  • Is there one clear professional story or direction running through it?
  • Did I include enough detail to make that story specific?
  • Am I adding context instead of repeating my resume?
  • Is every detail accurate?
